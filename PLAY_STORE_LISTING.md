# Verve: Daily Motivation & Inspiration

## App Name
Verve

## Short Description (max 80 chars)
Verve: Your daily dose of high-energy inspiration and motivational quotes.

## Full Description
**Ignite Your Inner Fire with Verve!**

Start every day with a surge of positivity and purpose. **Verve** is not just a quotes app; it’s your personal catalyst for growth, resilience, and daily inspiration. Designed with a high-energy, vibrant Material 3 aesthetic, Verve delivers powerful wisdom from the world's greatest minds directly to your fingertips.

Whether you're looking for a morning boost, a midday pick-me-up, or a reminder of your own strength, Verve is here to keep you moving forward. Our seamless, interactive experience ensures that motivation is always just a tap away.

### Why Verve?
*   **Daily Featured Quote:** Kickstart your journey with a handpicked, curated quote that updates every 24 hours to set the tone for your day.
*   **Tap-to-Refresh:** Need more? Simply tap the screen to instantly discover a new random quote from our extensive library. Unlimited inspiration, whenever you need it.
*   **Vibrant Material 3 Design:** Experience a modern, expressive UI that adapts to your style with dynamic color support (Android 12+) and a stunning, high-energy gradient aesthetic.
*   **Seamless Edge-to-Edge Display:** Lose yourself in the message with an immersive, distraction-free layout that uses every pixel of your screen.
*   **Resilient Offline Mode:** Inspiration shouldn't depend on your connection. Access your recently cached quotes and a curated set of bundled fallbacks even when you're off the grid.

## Key Features
*   **Curated Daily Inspiration:** Automatically updated every 24 hours.
*   **Instant Refresh:** Tap anywhere to fetch a new random quote.
*   **Expressive UI:** Full Material 3 support with dynamic light and dark themes.
*   **Immersive Layout:** Full edge-to-edge display for a modern, clean look.
*   **Smart Caching:** Room-powered local database for offline access.
*   **Zero Setup:** No accounts, no keys, no distractions. Just pure motivation.

## Target Audience
Anyone seeking daily motivation, personal growth, and a positive mindset. Perfect for students, professionals, athletes, and dreamers.
